---
title: Accessibility
description: Accessibility support and how to report a wiki issue.
---

Last updated September 24, 2026. We aim to make the wiki usable with a keyboard, screen reader, zoom, and reduced-motion settings.

Starlight provides a skip link, semantic documentation navigation, and keyboard-accessible search. Screenshot previews can be opened and closed with a keyboard. The sidebar toggle exposes its current state to assistive technology.

We have not commissioned a formal WCAG conformance audit, so we do not claim full compliance. If you encounter a barrier, [report it in the wiki repository](https://github.com/rwetz/nexis-wiki/issues) with the page, browser, and assistive technology you used. Avoid posting sensitive personal details in a public issue.
