Last updated September 24, 2026. This notice covers the wiki website, not the Nexis desktop app.

## Requests and storage

This is a static documentation site hosted by GitHub Pages. We do not run analytics or advertising scripts, require sign-in, or set tracking cookies in the wiki code. GitHub Pages receives ordinary web requests and may process technical data such as your IP address under the [GitHub privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

Search uses a locally served Pagefind index. The site's theme, keyboard shortcut display, and collapsed sidebar preference may be saved in your browser's `localStorage`. Starlight also saves some sidebar state in `sessionStorage`. These preferences stay in your browser and can be cleared through its site-data settings. Screenshot previews run in your browser and do not upload images.

There is no optional analytics or advertising storage to consent to, so we do not show a cookie banner. If that changes, this notice and any required consent control will be updated.

## Other sites and contact

Following links to [GitHub](https://github.com/rwetz/Nexis) or [nexisdev.org](https://nexisdev.org/privacy/) takes you to services with their own privacy practices. For a privacy question, [open an issue in the wiki repository](https://github.com/rwetz/nexis-wiki/issues). Avoid putting private information in a public issue.
